How to get a Square payment link
  1. Open the Square app or log in at squareup.com.
  2. Find Payment links (in the Square Dashboard it's under Online) and create a new link.
  3. Name it after your event and set the price of one ticket. If Square lets buyers change the amount or quantity, turn that on so people can buy more than one.
  4. Copy the link (it starts with https://square.link/) and paste it in the box above.

Stripe and PayPal payment links work the same way. Buyers tap Pay by debit or credit card and pay on Square's secure page, and the money goes to your Square account.

Privacy policy and terms

Last updated November 14, 2026

This site is a free community listing of cigar events. By using it, you agree to these terms.

Who can use it

  • You must be 21 or older to create a profile, post an event or buy tickets.
  • Events listed here are for adults 21 and over.

What we collect

  • Profiles: your name, email and password (the password is stored securely by our login provider, Supabase, and we can't see it).
  • Tickets: the name and email you give, how many tickets and the total. The event's host sees these so they can confirm your payment, check you in and email you about the event.
  • Things you post: events, photos, flyers and comments. These are public.
  • Saved events and searches: kept with your profile so you can find them again. If you save an event, its host can see your name and email so they can send you updates about it.
  • On your device: your color and text choices, and the ticket codes you bought without a profile.

We don't sell your information or show ads based on it. We don't handle your card number. Payments go straight to the host through the payment service they chose (like Square, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal or Zelle), and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Tickets and payments

  • Each event is run by its host, not by this site. The host sets the price, gets the money, and handles refunds, changes and cancellations.
  • Your tickets show "Waiting for payment" until the host marks them paid. Put your ticket code in the payment note so the host can match it.
  • Questions about an event or a refund go to the host, using the contact on the event page.

Rules for posts and comments

  • Only post real events with a real date and place, and only post photos you have the right to use.
  • No hate, harassment, adult or illegal content, spam, scams or misleading prices.
  • We may hide or remove any post, comment or profile that breaks these rules, without notice. Use "Report this event" on any event page to tell us about a problem.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your events and comments at any time.
  • You can remove your profile from your profile page. That deletes your saved events, saved searches and comments.
  • To ask for a copy of your information or have it deleted, contact the site owner.

No guarantees

The site is provided as is. We aren't responsible for events, hosts, payments or anything that happens at an event. Hosts are responsible for following local laws, including age and tobacco rules.

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
